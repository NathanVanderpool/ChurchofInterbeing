What

Sacred community
ritual

Weekly gatherings to reconnect with the living pulse of life — in ourselves, in each other, and in the wider whole.

How

Meditate, sing,
move, dialogue

Each gathering weaves contemplative practice with embodied presence, communal song and spacious conversation.

Where

Genezarethkirche,
Schillerkiez

A beloved community church in Neukölln, Berlin — and other places across the city, Europe and the world.

A community member speaks

"There is a hunger for genuine community, for ritual, for something sacred — it doesn't go away even for folks who have left religion behind. Church of Interbeing is for anyone who wants to reconnect with what actually matters: showing up fully, being seen, and feeling part of something larger than themselves. That's why we gather."

Nathan Vanderpool — musician & host

What

Sacred community
ritual

Weekly gatherings to reconnect with the living pulse of life — in ourselves, in each other, and in the wider whole.

How

Meditate, sing,
move, dialogue

Each gathering weaves contemplative practice with embodied presence, communal song and spacious conversation.

Where

Genezarethkirche,
Schillerkiez

A beloved community church in Neukölln, Berlin — and other places across the city, Europe and the world.

Why we gather
"As humans, we are sustained and animated by connection with ourselves, each other, and the wider wholes we are part of. With it, a doorway opens to a more beautiful world."
